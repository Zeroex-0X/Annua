First public release of Annua, a private reminder app for birthdays, anniversaries and events.

**What you get**
- Birthdays, anniversaries and custom events, each with a photo, relationship and category
- Calendar view that highlights every day with an event
- Reminders as a notification or a full-screen alarm, on the day or any number of days before
- Your own sounds for notifications, birthday alarms and event alarms
- Several phone numbers and accounts per person, with country codes, and one-tap calling, messaging and social links
- Backup and restore to a file on your phone
- Light, dark or automatic theme, pure black mode and accent colours
- Settings, then App update, tells you when a new version is out and what changed

**Install**
Download `Annua.apk` below and open it. If Android asks, allow installing from your browser or file manager. Then open Annua and allow notifications, exact alarms and unrestricted battery so reminders arrive on time.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
